01 · Brand

Play above the city

Estancia SkyCourts is rooftop pickleball at Estancia, Capitol Commons, in Ortigas: covered and open-air courts with the city on the horizon and a lounge for after the game.

The brand should feel like a club more than a sports facility. Every piece of communication should carry five qualities:

Lifestyle. The game is part of a good day, not the whole of it.
Elevated. Literally up there. Calm, confident, never loud.
Social. Open play, rotating partners, regulars.
Club. Members-first in tone, welcoming to guests.
Scenic. Sky, skyline and light do a lot of the work.

In practice: generous space, one display face used large, a quiet palette taken from the venue itself, and a single gesture, the S-curve from the logo, used sparingly.

02 · Logo

The logo

One logo, always used whole: the ESTANCIA line above the SKYCOURTS wordmark, with the ball and the S-swoosh running through the O.

The logo is a single colour. The ball and swoosh are never picked out in a second colour.

Clear space

x x x x

Keep a margin of x on every side, where x is the diameter of the ball (about 4.5% of the logo’s width). Nothing else goes inside that space: no text, edges, folds or busy photo detail.

Minimum size

  • Print: 40 mm wide. Below that, the hairlines in the S, C and ESTANCIA line break up.
  • Screen: 160 px wide.
  • Embroidery: 70 mm wide, or use the logomark alone (section 03).
  • Anything smaller uses the logomark or the app icon.

04 · Colour

Court & Teak

Every colour is taken from the venue itself: the court surface, the teak slats, the travertine walls and the light over the city.

Proportion

Roughly half of any layout is Travertine or Linen, a third is the blues, Teak adds warmth, and gold stays scarce so it reads as premium.

Readable pairings

SampleText on backgroundContrastUse for

Hex and RGB values are for screens. For print, match each colour to a Pantone or CMYK value on a physical proof with your printer, and check Teak and Court Navy against the real decking and court samples.

06 · Graphic element

The S-curve

The swoosh in the logo is the brand’s one graphic gesture. Used as an edge, it cuts layouts the way the skyline cuts the sky.

  • Use the curve once per layout, at the bottom. It never frames the top or sides.
  • The field is a single solid colour: Court Navy, or Travertine on dark layouts.
  • Text inside the field is right-aligned, where the field is tallest.
  • No gradients, stacked bands or drop shadows on the field.

08 · Photography

Natural, unfiltered

  • Use photos in natural colour. Correct exposure and white balance only.
  • Real play and real people: mid-rally, between points, at the lounge. Avoid stiff, posed group shots.
  • Shoot with headroom. Leave sky or a quiet wall above the subject for the logo and headline.
  • The headline takes whichever of Court Navy or Linen reads best against that part of the photo.
  • Never put text over a busy area of a photo. Move it into the S-curve field instead.
  • Favour golden hour and evening light. The skyline and sky are part of the brand.

Social posts need at least 1080 × 1350. Print and website heroes need originals of at least 3000 px on the long side.
